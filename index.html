What is Navratri

Nine nights of the Divine Mother

Navratri honours Maa in her many forms — strength, wisdom, abundance, grace. Nine nights of worship, music and surrender, culminating in light.

Through a Devotee's Eyes

"I came as a traveller.
I left as a child of the Mother."

The heart of this yatra isn't the places you'll see — it's who you become in her presence. Every devotee returns carrying a little more of her light.

A Day in the Yatra

Before You Go

Cultural considerations

🧕

Dress

Modest, comfortable clothing. Shoulders and knees covered at temples.

🙏

Temple etiquette

Shoes removed, quiet reverence, follow the priests' guidance.

🕉️

Customs

Right hand for offerings; a gentle, respectful presence throughout.

📷

Photography

Always ask first — some spaces are sacred and camera-free.

Take Care of You

Health & wellbeing

💊

Medications

Bring a full supply plus copies of prescriptions in your carry-on.

💉

Vaccinations

Check current recommendations with your GP or a travel clinic.

💧

Stay well

Bottled water, gentle pacing, and rest when your body asks.

🩺

Travel cover

Comprehensive travel & medical insurance is strongly advised.

On the Ground · 2026

Practicalities

📱

Connectivity

An eSIM or local SIM keeps you online affordably from day one.

💵

Money

Carry some cash for temples & markets; cards work in cities.

🔌

Power

India uses Type C/D/M plugs, 230V — bring a universal adapter.

🎒

Pack light

Layers, a shawl for temples, and good walking shoes.
